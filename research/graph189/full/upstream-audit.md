- The local PDF of Easwar–Johari agrees with arXiv v1. Theorem B covers diameter at most two, order at most $2\sigma+2$, 2-connected graphs of order $2\sigma+3$, and graphs that are not 2-connected. Sections 3–5 use path closure and block decomposition. No theorem there handles the full graph of diameter three, four or five in this residual range. The transversal bound underlying external PR #70 is reusable as a handwritten lemma. Fix a vertex $x$, choose a predecessor $q(z)$ at even distance from $x$ for each $z$ at odd distance at least three from $x$, and choose a minimum set $Y\subseteq N(x)$ meeting the geodesic-first-step sets for these $q(z)$. Every $y\in Y$ has a private selected predecessor. For a fixed $y'\in Y$, charge each other $y\in Y$ to $y$ if $yy'$ is absent and to its private predecessor if $yy'$ is present. In the second case the path through $y$ and the privacy condition force even distance from $y'$. These witnesses are injective and outside the odd outer set. Thus each $y'$ can cover at most $\sigma+1-|Y|$ odd outer vertices, proving $|O(x)|\le |Y|(\sigma+1-|Y|)$, with $|Y|\le \deg(x)$.

## Exact criteria for the full OMA proof

All three exact leaves below assume finite connected nontrivial simple graphs, $\delta+\sigma+1<n\le(\delta+1)(\sigma+1)$, the original even-distance cap, and $|I|\le|N(I)|$ for every independent $I$. The first two additionally assume a triangle.

| Exact leaf | Remaining parameters in its statement |
|---|---|
| `R1.H1.R1.H1` | Diameter three, minimum degree at most two |
| `R1.H1.R1.H2` | Diameter three, minimum degree at least three |
| `R1.H2` | Diameter four or five, and every distance-five pair has an endpoint of degree less than $\sigma$ |

For a paper-level proof, the Easwar–Johari theorem can be applied at each relevant leaf. The degree-two order-fourteen proof at $\sigma=5$ settles only that sector. The handwritten diameter-five exclusion at $\delta\le3$ settles only part of the third leaf. The argument for triangle-free graphs does not leave a separate four-cycle branch.

For a kernel proof through the OMA reductions, prove each whole leaf with its exact hypotheses and combine the two triangle-bearing proofs with `R1.H1.R1.main`, then combine the resulting diameter-three proof with `R1.H2` using `R1.main`. Alternatively directly prove `type_of% @WrittenOnTheWallII.GraphConjecture189.conjecture189`. Citing Easwar–Johari does not supply a Lean term for any case that invokes it. Any computational exclusion needs a kernel-verifiable translation and complete, independently replayable certificate. The final dependency audit should contain only the standard Lean axioms and no use of the upstream placeholder.

For a disproof, one finite simple connected nontrivial graph must satisfy the original cap and fail to have a Hamiltonian path. A search finding models of a branch's premises without checking nontraceability, or showing a strategy has exceptions, supplies no counterexample.
