# Graph 189 audit for one all-visible remote

Use the degree-three triangle, d=1, singleton-private setup. Write t for the only all-visible remote, S=N(t)∩C, s=|S|, and q=|N(t)∩{p_1,p_2,p_3}|. Each common middle vertex has exactly one private neighbor and is adjacent to t and all other common middle vertices. Each private is represented.

The bound s≥4 in triangle17/graph189.md checks. If s=3, the earlier estimates force q=1. The anchors and two missed privates consume five of t's distance-two slots. Thus H={t}∪S∪(N₂(t)∩C) has size at most k−1. Every member x of S has all its remote neighbors in H. Its degree requires at least two private neighbors, so x is pair-visible and has all H minus itself as remote neighbors. If x omits class h, x can reach every remote outside U_h in at most two steps, via its two privates or via adjacency to t. At most two of those can lie outside H, since x sees all k−4 common middle vertices via t and its two anchors via its privates. This implies u_h≥k−3. A common representative of h sees all of S and U_h at distance two, as well as z and the two other privates. These sets are disjoint because all S are pair-visible. Therefore u_h≤k−6, contradiction.

Generally |H|≤k+s+q−5. If x∈S has r_x∈{1,2} private neighbors, it has at least k−r_x remote neighbors and hence misses at most s+q−6+r_x other vertices in H. A pair-visible x∈S omitting h forces c−u_h≤|H|+2. At the representative of h, u_h+s−|S∩U_h|≤k−3. The two inequalities imply |S∩U_h|≥c−2k+6−q≥4−q.

If s=4, q=0 is impossible. Every pair-visible member would require four additional exclusive members, while an exclusive member requires s+q≥5 by its remote degree. For s=4 and q=1, some member of S is exclusive, since every pair-visible member requires three exclusive members. Such an exclusive x meets all of H other than itself, with |H|=k. The external C boundary O of H then has size at most three, since x sees all W and its anchor at distance two. If c=2k−2, each non-boundary vertex outside H has degree at most k−1, so c≥2k−1. Then no member of S is pair-visible, since it would require four additional exclusive members. If c=2k−1, at least k−4 external non-boundary vertices must be pair-visible by degree. But summing the three representative caps gives |E|≤3k−8−c−2s=k−15, contradiction. Thus s=4,q=1 forces c≥2k.
