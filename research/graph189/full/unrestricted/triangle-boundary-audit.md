# The triangle-transversal boundary k=3t+2

Independently checked triangle-boundary.md SHA-256 f84760b3800104a690f51665090eeffd37f6751ff253aa1d80bfced2aaf01c5f. The boundary proof and the updated packing and transversal bounds pass.

Failure of transitivity gives disjoint E sets A,B and an intersecting bridge F. At r=k−t=2t+2, the upper bound on |F| is exactly 2(t+1)=r. The derivation forces both outer cardinalities to be r, both unions to reach k+1, and the cover F⊆(F−A)∪(F−B) to be a disjoint cover; the latter means F⊆A∪B. Both intersections have size t+1. Because each of the three neighborhood indices is ambient-high and has exactly r neighbors in H, it is adjacent to all of X and has degree exactly k.

For a member a of F on the A side, the two incident indices witness every other member of A∪F at distance two; adjacency between a and any such peer would give a triangle in H. Since |A∪F|=k+1, this is the full even set of a including itself. Symmetrically a B-side a has B∪F as its even set. No such vertex can have a distance-four vertex (the whole even set has distances zero or two), hence no vertex at any distance ≥4 by a geodesic. Each a is adjacent to all of X; otherwise b, the middle index, would provide an extra distance-two witness in X.

For q at distance three from b, consider any geodesic b,u,p,q. If u∈F its saturated even set already contains q. Otherwise u∈X since N(b)=F∪X. The vertex p, at distance two from b, is outside F. As (A∪F)∩(B∪F)=F, at least one of these two even sets omits p. Choose an a∈F with exactly that even set. It is adjacent to u; a,u,p puts p within distance two from a and p is outside its entire even set, forcing adjacency. Now q is within two steps from a and cannot be a or its immediate neighbor since d(b,q)=3 and ab is an edge. Hence q is in a's even set and in Ω=A∪B. Thus N₃(b)⊆Ω−F. The eccentricity of b is ≤4 by using any adjacent a∈F. Counting one center, k neighbors, ≤k positive-even vertices, and ≤2t+2= k−t third-layer vertices yields n≤3k+1−t≤3k for t≥1. The older independently audited n≤3k order theorem suffices. The nontransitive case need not yield bipartiteness; this is a distinct order argument.

With the formerly verified transitive/bipartite case this proves traceability for any triangle transversal of size t with k≥3t+2. A counterexample therefore has minimum vertex triangle transversal ≥ceil((k−1)/3). A maximal packing of m vertex-disjoint triangles covers all triangles with its 3m vertices, giving m≥ceil((k−1)/9). The separate prior triangle-free result gives at least one triangle for every counterexample.
