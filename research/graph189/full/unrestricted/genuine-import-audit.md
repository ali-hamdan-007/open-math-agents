A check against a focused local interface is a different verification from the original dependency or official CI.

The authors' 21-page paper is Rohun Easwar and Shaurya Johari, *Conditions for traceability under a bound on the size of even-distance sets*, arXiv:2609.11719. In the available original PDF, Theorem B(4) covers all qualifying connected graphs that are not 2-connected. Theorem 5.11 is the section-level statement proving the same implication. Lemma 3.1 is the traceability edge-closure rule with degree sum at least n−1. Lemma 5.10 concerns a graph with a pendant marker t at c and requires the graph minus t to be 2-connected in addition to its metric and degree conditions. The present direct two-branch argument uses the checked two-side bounds and path closure. It does not need to establish 2-connectivity on each augmented side or invoke Lemma 5.10 as a black box.
