# Independent audit of the triangle witness and chordal consequence

Audited maximal-triangle.md SHA-256 966cd45982c4eabf33233924444e1c57fcdcdabbb58bc06c50e87d1be03a9079. The metric witness and maximal-clique corollary both pass.

Across the three vertices of a clique, the distances from x differ by at most one. If all three are odd they are the same odd integer. Thus the absence of the indicated witness makes their three self-inclusive even sets cover V. At n=3k+3 the cover saturates the three caps of k+1 and is a partition. For a vertex with distance one to the triangle, its three distances lie in {1,2} with at least one 1. Exactly one can be 2, so it is adjacent to precisely two members of the triangle. If any vertex had distance two to the triangle, a predecessor of distance one touches two triangle members; both are then exactly two away, violating disjointness. Connectedness excludes larger distances as well. The resulting dominating triangle gives n≤1+δ+2k≤3k+1 by the separately checked dominating-clique proof, a contradiction. Thus n≤3k+2. A distance-one common witness would form a four-clique, validating the distance ≥3 conclusion for a maximal triangle.

For a connected chordal graph, if a clique K lies entirely in layer r≥1 from x, it is contained in one component C of the vertices of distance at least r. Its external neighbor set S lies in layer r−1. If r=1 then S is contained in {x}; otherwise any two nonadjacent boundary vertices admit an induced path with internal vertices in C, and a second induced path with internal vertices in layers ≤r−2. Existence follows from connectedness of C and routes to x respectively; shortest paths under those restrictions ensure no chord from endpoints to an interior of the same path. No cross chord between the interiors exists because the distance layers differ by ≥2. This contradicts chordality, so S is a clique. Every K vertex has a geodesic predecessor in S. Their nonempty S-neighborhoods are pairwise comparable: incomparable witnesses and the adjacent K pair would induce a four-cycle. The smallest among the finite nested sets contains a single vertex adjacent to all of K, disallowing maximality. A putative x in K is automatically not equidistant to K when |K|≥2.

Consequently a qualifying connected chordal graph with an inclusion-maximal triangle falls in the verified bounded-order theorem; a maximal edge gives the even cover by two sets, and the singleton case is immediate. Treewidth at most two is one valid corollary. This does not remove the remaining chordal case where every maximal clique has at least four vertices.
