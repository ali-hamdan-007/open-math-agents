If minimum-degree x meets center c of eccentricity at most two, N[c] is contained in the radius-two ball of x. Hence d(c)≤δ+|N₂(x)|. Together with n=1+d(c)+|N₂(c)| this gives n≤1+δ+2k. In the positive-even version, subtract the exact count of x's distance-four-or-greater even witnesses from its distance-two allowance; none is counted in N₂(x). If x itself is a center there is the more direct n≤1+δ+k. Every vertex of a dominating clique has eccentricity at most two, and a minimum-degree vertex either belongs to that clique or meets it. Under the Graph189 hypothesis the bound yields traceability. In fact the previously established n≤δ+2k+2 clause in /tmp/full189nov28/split-ball/strong-order.md already suffices directly, even without invoking all of the n≤3k+2 theorem.

In the subtree intersection representation of a chordal graph, the union of every vertex's closed neighborhood of subtrees is connected because each meets the center vertex's subtree. Diameter at most three ensures the unions for any two vertices intersect at a common subtree or an intersection of adjacent representing subtrees. The finite-tree Helly argument is correct: for two intersecting subtrees their nearest-to-root nodes are ancestors of the intersection node, so the deepest nearest node chosen across all subtrees belongs to each one. Cliqueness of subtrees containing it and domination by this clique follow directly.

For the four-clique path blowup with sizes 2,k−1,k,k, the four degrees are k, 2k, 3k−2, 2k−1 respectively (the second value is 2+(k−2)+k=2k). Thus two vertices have degree k and all others are at least k at all k≥2. The positive even classes for the four positions have sizes k,k,2,k−1, totaling at most k individually, and the diameter is exactly three. Middle-clique union is a dominating clique; vertices in either outer clique are simplicial, and the remaining two middle cliques form a clique. Total order 3k+1 attains the proved bound, including at k=2. The explicitly ordered clique traversal is a Hamilton path. No part of this argument requires identifying this family with another blowup construction.
