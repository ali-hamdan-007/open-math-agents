# A bounded number of exceptional degree-sized cells at every large minimum cut

In any sequence meeting the necessary counterexample order and size bounds, with connectivity s tending to infinity and A,B the two sides of a minimum cut S, A the smaller, the following conclusions hold. No assumption that either side contains a nonport is made, and the exceptional low vertex may be anywhere. There are s−O(1) pairwise disjoint dense sets in B, at most one per anchor, each satisfying the hypotheses of cyclable-dense-blocks.md. Together they leave O(k) vertices outside their union in all of G. In particular a single cycle misses only O(k) vertices. The constants are absolute, with k the ambient second-smallest degree. This improves the global dense-block decomposition at this cut from a sublinear uncovered proportion to O(1/s), and works when the small side is completely covered by its separator neighbors.

Use n≥(s+1)(k−s+2)−1, s²=O(k), and |A|=(1+o(1))k from the previously audited bounds. Also |A|≥k−s+1, since it contains a high vertex. Write Q_i=N_B(i), P_i=N_A(i), q_i=|Q_i|, p_i=|P_i|, and R_i=N_B(Q_i) outside Q_i. For any high reference point a∈A put T=N_S(a). Associate the B set E_i=R_i to i∈T and E_i=Q_i to i outside T. Each E_i has order at most k, by the cap at i (for R_i) or any opposite contact (for Q_i).

Every high b∈B at odd distance from a belongs to the union of these E_i. The distance is three. If its first step enters anchor i∈T and the next step is in B, then b∈R_i, as b cannot itself touch i. If its first B point is b itself, the preceding point is some anchor j not touching a, so b∈Q_j with j outside T. These cover all geodesic forms other than first passing from a to a neighbor in A and then to an anchor, which is also included in the second alternative. Thus at most k+1 B vertices are outside the union, allowing a single low exception. The exact debit including repetitions is
    F_a=sk−|union E_i|=Σ_i(k−|E_i|)+Σ_{b in union E_i} (#E_i containing b−1)
       ≤ sk−|B|+k+1 ≤ s²+|A| ≤ (2+o(1))k.
For another high a′∈A, the Q sets at labels in T_a′ outside T belong to this family; they all give even witnesses at a′. Their union has at most k members and at least |T_a′−T|k−F_a. Consequently every high a′ has at most three labels outside T and, by reversing the references, misses at most three labels of T. This alignment is uniform in the reference point. A possible low point costs at most s incidence exceptions; hence
    Σ_{j outside T}p_j=O(k),   Σ_{i in T}(k−p_i)=O(k).
For the second assertion use |T|(k−|A|)≤s² and the just-proved bound on total missed labels in T. The deficits Σ_{j outside T}(k−q_j) and Σ_{i in T}(k−|R_i|) are O(k) directly by F_a.

A further bound, despite possible repetitions among the Q_i for i∈T, is
    Σ_{i in T} q_i=O(k).
Indeed their union U has at most k members by the even cap at a. Put d_i=k−p_i. Every q∈Q_i has at most d_i even-distance witnesses in B, and misses at most d_i members of Q_i besides itself. For each b∈R_i choose its adjacent q₀∈Q_i. At least q_i−d_i members of Q_i lie within two steps of b, via q₀ or by adjacency. Summing and subtracting the available positive-even incidences from Q_i gives
    e(Q_i,R_i) ≥ (q_i−d_i)|R_i|−q_i d_i.
Across all i∈T the sum of these edge counts is ≤|U|·5k+|U|F_a=O(k²). Indeed each oriented pair (u,b), u∈U, with b in the R union is charged at most once plus the repeated occurrences of b in those R sets. Every vertex has degree ≤5k by the degree-k root and adjacent-degree-difference bound of ../order/direct-block-links.md. Finally the total error against kΣq_i from the right side of the last display is bounded by kΣ_i(k−|R_i|)+2kΣ_i d_i=O(k²). This proves the bound.

We construct provisional sets V_i, using only high B members. At j outside T take Q_j with the possible low member removed. Except for at most one j with only low A contacts, its B boundary is at most p_j+s−1: the closed internal neighborhood of a high A contact leaves at least k−s+1−p_j even points from j inside A. The possible exceptional j can be discarded entirely for cost O(k); two such j would violate Hall on the minimum cut, as |A|>s. Thus each V_j has full boundary O(p_j+s), and |k−|V_j||≤(k−q_j)+1. The removals cost at most one target point per occurrence.

At i in T choose a high u_i∈Q_i. If none exists, discard the label, again costing O(k), at most once by Hall on B. Take V_i=N_B[u_i] with any low member removed. Its original high center has internal B degree ≥k−s. All of its neighbors lie in Q_i∪R_i, so
    k−s ≤ |V_i| ≤ k+q_i.
Every B boundary point of the untrimmed set is at distance two from u_i, outside its already charged P_i. Thus the full boundary after the optional low removal is at most s+(k−p_i)+1. We also have |V_i minus R_i|≤q_i and |R_i minus V_i|≤q_i+s. It follows from the four summed estimates that
    Σ_i [ ||V_i|−k| + |external boundary of V_i| + |V_i symmetric-difference E_i| ] = O(k)
over retained labels, since s²=O(k). The discarded targets cost only O(k) as explained. Since the E_i have only O(k) total repetitions and cover B except for O(k) vertices, the same is true of the provisional V_i.

Order the retained sets in any way and trim from each all members already present in earlier original sets. The total number trimmed across all sets is the total repeated incidence count, hence O(k). The new sets are disjoint and their total boundary increases by at most this many. For each set let its cost be its absolute size difference from k and its full exterior boundary size. The total cost remains O(k). Discard the sets with cost >k/20; there are O(1) and each costs O(k) omitted points (every provisional set has ≤2k vertices). Each surviving set has m≥19k/20 vertices and boundary b≤k/20. All its points are originally high, so internal minimum degree ≥k−b, and the internal missing parameter counting self is at most d=m−k+b≤k/10. Consequently m≥2d+3(b+1)+1 for large k. There are s−O(1) such sets and at most s; cyclable-dense-blocks.md supplies a single cycle covering every one. All left-out points, including A and S, total O(k).
