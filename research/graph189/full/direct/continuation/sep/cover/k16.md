# Traceability at second-smallest degree sixteen

Every qualifying Graph 189 graph of second-smallest degree 16 has a spanning path. Connectivity at most three or at least six was settled previously. The following bounds assume only s≥4 and therefore treat both remaining connectivities at once. Use the exterior setup from k14-k15.md and the geodesic order upper n≤98. Here h=8.

In a 2-connected near-Hamilton-connected exterior C of order m≤2q−1, its contact matching number j≥4 has 3j<16. At j=5 the local exterior-size bound m≥16−5+3=14 implies n≥7·14+4=102. At j=4 use the refined size result ../../verify/near-hc-exterior-size.md. It gives 2r≥j+1 for r=m−1+j−16, so m≥16 and n≥6·16+3=99. Both are impossible.

For an endblock B of order b≤2q−2 the noncut-to-P matching J is at least max(3,17−b), with b≥9. The bound n≥(J+2)b+23 leaves only b=14 or 15, with J=3. These configurations have exactly the structure of b=k−2 or b=k−1 endblocks in k14-k15.md: the former has all noncuts forming a clique complete to a four-element boundary, the latter gives either this same clique after putting another vertex into the boundary, or a set of high noncuts missing at most one neighbor within that set and the four boundary vertices. In every case some high member has degree at most 17 and a second-layer cover of size at most four. The root order bound gives n≤1+17+16+4·13=86. This contradicts n≥93.

Finally take a 2-connected whole C=B with b≥2q or an endblock B with b≥2q−1. The earlier cycle packing gives n≥9q+7 in either case, ruling out q≥11. At q=8 the optimized high x has at most one distance-two gateway in B, an impossibility at b≥15. At q=10 its ambient second layer inside C has size r≤5. There exists a high vertex inside B outside H=N_C[x], R and the possible single block exception (since b≥19>11+5+1). All its q block neighbors are outside H, so b≥2q+2=22. In a whole C the original cycle count used only b≥2q and improves by two to n≥99. For an endblock the ordinary larger endblock packing bound 9q+h+4 already exceeds 98.

This leaves q=9, r=2 or 3. The r≤1 cases are excluded by the same one-gateway argument. Apply the direct-contact tail estimate in k14-k15.md with m₀=20 for the whole and m₀=28 for an endblock. At r=3 all C vertices are high, |H*|=10, J≥5 and the whole-tail bonus is at least 9−3+2=8, giving n≥20+5+6·10+2·8=101. In an endblock the possible loss of one on each tail is outweighed by the eight extra exterior vertices. At r=2 we have J≥6, |H*|≥9, and each tail gains at least 9−2+1=8 even without any further credit. Thus n≥20+6+7·9+16=105. All configurations are excluded.
