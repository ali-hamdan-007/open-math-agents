# Traceability at second-smallest degree ten

Every connected graph satisfying Graph 189 with second-smallest degree k=10 has a spanning path. With the earlier order case and the unrestricted connectivity-three theorem, we may assume κ≥4 and 48≤n≤51. The upper bound follows from n≤1+δ+k+floor((k+1)²/4)=δ+41. Thus at order 51 every vertex has degree at least ten.

Take a longest path P with a high-bearing exterior component C as in the prior almost-spanning and exterior-degree results; let m=|C|. We have m≥7, and every high C vertex has at least h=5 C-neighbors. We use the established contact-pair inequality that a singleton low leaf attached only to one C neighbor would have ambient degree at most two, impossible here.

If C has a cut vertex, choose an endblock B with only high noncuts. Put b=|B|≥6 and let q≥5 be the minimum internal degree of those noncuts. The remainder C−B contains a high vertex by the preceding singleton observation (the sole alternative would be just that low). Hence |C−B|≥5, since a high member has at most the one articulation as an external C neighbor. If b≥2q−1, Fan's theorem gives a cycle of length at least nine in B. Four disjoint connectors between it and P exist. Both tails of P have at least nine vertices outside the marks, and all three inner gaps at least six by the longer cycle arc. Thus |P|≥40 and |C|≥14, impossible. Otherwise b≤2q−2, and B minus its articulation is Hamilton-connected. Its contact matching into P has size t≥max(3,11−b): a cover of size ≤2 together with the articulation would cut G, and each noncut has at least 11−b contacts. As in the preceding three-mark argument, each outer tail costs at least b+h−1 and each inner gap at least b−1. Together with |C|≥b+h this yields n≥(t+2)b+3h−1. At b=6,7 it is at least 56, and at b≥8 at least 54. Thus C is 2-connected.

Write q for the minimum internal degree of the high C members. If m≥2q then Fan gives a cycle of length ≥10, and the four-connector count gives n≥3·10+4+3(5+1)=52. Hence m≤2q−1. The degree-two-marker closure from the earlier exterior proof gives a spanning cycle of C, and a spanning path between any prescribed distinct ends except for at most one pair, the two internal neighbors of a low vertex having exactly two. Even that pair allows a path on m−1 vertices. Thus t distinct matching contacts to P require n≥(t+2)m+t−1. A matching of four exists by connectivity. It follows that m≤8. At m=8 equality would require n=51 and an exceptional low vertex; yet n=51 forces minimum degree ten. With no exception the lower bound is 6·8+4=52. Thus m=7. Five matched contacts would require 7·7+5−1=53 vertices. The matching number is exactly four. Every high C member needs at least four path contacts. A size-four minimum cover therefore consists of four marks, since a cover using any C vertex would leave another high C member uncovered with only three covered marks. Every high C member touches all four marks, is universal inside C to reach ten, and contributes no other exterior contact. The group C is a clique even if one member is low. The four marks S separate it from all other vertices.

Each realization of a longest path on V(P) has at least m unmarked vertices in each of the five gaps and tails determined by S. The argument uses the high clique members, so every pair of distinct marks supports a spanning C route even if one low vertex misses a mark. Among all attainable endpoints on V(P) there is a high endpoint e avoiding all four marks: the registered endpoint-set lower bound gives at least k+2 endpoints, and at most k can meet a mark as they would be even witnesses for any fixed high C vertex. Orient that realization starting at e. If σ denotes the total excess of those five pieces above 5m, then d(e)≤m−1+σ. Indeed e has no mark neighbor, and a neighbor in the first m positions of any piece after a mark would rotate e into an initial tail shorter than m, contradicting its registered lower bound. The initial piece contributes at most its order minus one. Hence σ≥4 and n≥m+4+5m+4=50. Only n=50 or 51 remain.

**Four-anchor elimination.** The independent finite support/count program clique4.py uses exact real linear arithmetic plus Boolean type indicators, relaxing the actual nonnegative integer multiplicities. There are exactly eleven unlabeled anchor graphs on four vertices. The high C clique witnesses the distance between distinct anchors as one or two according to that anchor graph. At n=51 the quadratic bound gives δ=10. At n=50 the only possible low vertex has degree nine, so a low C vertex has exactly three of the four contacts. Otherwise the degree spectrum is unrestricted.

Each outside vertex type specifies its side (C or the remainder B), low status and four distances to the marks. High/high pairs have distance at most four and all distances at most five. The sum along the best anchor is the exact distance on opposite sides. A high C vertex is adjacent to every mark, bounding B depth by three for high B vertices and four for a low B vertex. Triangle inequalities bound same-side pair distances from below by their largest coordinate difference and above by both a route through S and the pairwise diameter bound. Equality of those bounds can force even charges. Each vertex has enough permissible same-side neighbors, counting itself once in the sum of its own and other candidate type populations, and a one-step predecessor to each anchor along a shortest route. If that first step is an anchor, its actual distance to the destination suffices; otherwise it is required on the same side. The graph degree and even quotas hold also at every mark.

For a vertex x on either side T with t mark-neighbors, W=N_T[x] has at least four boundary vertices, since its deletion separates W from the other side. All of that boundary except its t adjacent anchors are own-side or mark vertices at distance two from x. Thus the count of opposite-side even witnesses plus forced own-side or mark fourth-layer vertices is at most k−4+t. Separately all opposite fourth-layer witnesses leave space for a second layer of size at least four. This argument does not require the remainder B to be connected. Every set of j marks touches at least 2j distinct B vertices: the immediate neighbors of each mark along P are distinct from those of all other marks by the gap lengths. These are all valid necessary conditions.

The 64 graph/order/low-location types in clique4.py comprise the two orders and the low anchor orbits under each anchor graph's automorphisms. Reproduce the primary run with `python run_four.py four-first.jsonl 3500 3`. It gives 63 UNSAT and one timeout. The final case `python clique4.py 222222 B 50 --timeout 60000` returns UNSAT, recorded in four-retry.jsonl. Thus no qualifying residual clique can occur. This proves the theorem.
