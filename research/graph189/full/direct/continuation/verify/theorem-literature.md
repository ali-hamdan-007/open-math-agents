# Literature used by the traceability proofs

* A. S. Hasratian and N. K. Khachatrian, “Some localization theorems on Hamiltonian circuits,” Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B 49 (1990), 287–294, Theorem 1. A connected graph of order at least three is Hamiltonian if for each induced u–w–v the sum of its endpoints' degrees is at least the size of the union of all three open neighborhoods. This is the exact L0 hypothesis used in the large-order part. The authors' names also appear as Asratian and Khachatryan. https://liu.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1161539/FULLTEXT01.pdf
* Geng-Hua Fan, “New sufficient conditions for cycles in graphs,” Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B 37 (1984), 221–227. If a 2-connected graph has max(d(x),d(y))≥c/2 at every pair of vertices at distance two, it is Hamiltonian or has a cycle of length at least c. Dirac's minimum-degree circumference bound is the direct special case with c=2δ. Original https://doi.org/10.1016/0095-8956(84)90054-6. Explicit statement as Theorem 1 in B. Ning, Applied Mathematics E-Notes 13 (2013), 208–211: https://www.math.nthu.edu.tw/~amen/2013/130223%28final%29.pdf
* J. A. Bondy and V. Chvátal, “A method in graph theory,” Discrete Mathematics 15 (1976), 111–135. Adding a nonedge whose endpoints have degree sum at least the graph order preserves Hamiltonicity in both directions. The degree-two marker used in the manuscripts reduces spanning paths with two prescribed endpoints to this exact form. https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(76)90078-9
* Reinhard Diestel, Graph Theory, third edition, Theorem 2.1.1 (König, maximum matching size equals minimum vertex-cover size in a bipartite graph), and Theorem 3.3.1 (Menger, vertex-disjoint connections between two vertex sets). https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/diestel1.pdf
* Rohun Easwar and Shaurya Johari, “Conditions for traceability under a bound on the size of even-distance sets,” arXiv:2609.11719 (2026), Theorem B and the surrounding discussion of Graph Conjecture 189. The newer bounds, finite checks, and separating-pair arguments used in this checkpoint are supplied by the research manuscripts cited in adjacent-degrees.md. https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11719v1
