## Geometric checks

* Across the separator the distance is precisely the minimum coordinate sum. On one side coordinate differences lower-bound the distance, while paths through any single anchor and the applicable diameter upper-bound it. Positive-even budgets count disjoint actual vertices and all their multiplicities.
* A vertex with an opposite fourth-layer witness has at least three distinct vertices in its second layer by connectivity. The outside boundary of the closed internal neighborhood has at least three vertices: another side lies beyond it, since no vertex there is adjacent to this neighborhood. Every boundary member other than the root's own anchor contacts is at distance exactly two in the root's side or the anchor set. These charges are disjoint from the forced same-side and anchor fourth-layer terms as well as the opposite-side terms.
* In the large-side branch, if a step from the small side lowers two anchor coordinates, it and one step for the third coordinate cover the large side using three even quotas. Thus a single internal step can lower at most one coordinate. The induced sum-of-positive-coordinate difference bound guarantees disjoint closed internal neighborhoods when it exceeds two, including when an ambient route through anchors is shorter. If two anchors are adjacent, their small-side port sets coincide, and connectivity forces every small-side vertex to touch both. A nonport excludes that case in the deep calculation. The narrow-order calculation correctly retains adjacent anchors.
* If the only nonport of one side is the sole low vertex z, a high vertex at depth at least three on the other side forces all high vertices on z's side to distance four plus a disjoint second layer of size three. The degree test forces that side to have exactly k−2 members and its high members to be universal in the side and to all anchors. All vertices opposite are high. Opposite depths one and three are even from such a high member, and depth two is even from z. There are no higher depths. Thus the opposite side has at most 2k vertices, below the required order. The general low depth-three ceiling follows from the high nonport on the side opposite the low vertex.
* In the exterior-clique calculation, the other vertices outside the three marks may have multiple components. Every cross path still visits a mark, the closed-neighborhood boundary still separates from a nonempty opposite side, and same-side distance upper bounds remain ambient. All anchormark distance triangles are permutations of 111,112,122,222. The distinct path brackets supply two distinct opposite contacts per mark, including the stated union inequality on every mark subset.
* The archive deliberately retains all three missing-anchor choices on the small side after normalizing the large-side absence, so swapping equal anchor coordinates and relabelling a low anchor loses no case. Direct labelled enumeration verified coverage.

Consequently the small calculation completes traceability for n≤5k−3 at all k using the earlier analytic reductions. The second calculation combines with the shallow-side, three-sole-nearest, moderate-order and cut-component theorems to settle connectivity exactly three for every k≥16.

## All degrees at connectivity at most three

The additional small_three_types.py family for k=9,…,15 passed all three separate checks. Its source SHA256 is ee9fdd40f6368653d71ca2a18b7d19e26e25a643e2095c3d2595cc5fa3363d76. The archived manifest SHA256 is 8230e93b22ba65398085d8a2128f873e3bc886b9d24cf674cd6b39d87c209484.

2. A fresh solve of each index returned UNSAT. The individual decisions, independent run script and log are in verify/replay/small-k-fresh.jsonl, independent-small.py and independent-small.log. All 201 initial timeouts in the originating computation also had UNSAT terminal records.
3. The supplied k=10 double-support graph was accepted. Independently constructed 22-vertex examples have a K9 side with eight triple ports and one nonport, a K10 side with seven triple ports and three nonports, and three anchors. Their unique low has degree eight, second degree nine and maximum positive-even population eight. Both the independent-anchor and one-anchor-edge versions had their true BFS populations accepted after deleting exactly the global order lower bound. See small-low-control.py and its log.

The least side A is a component, and B may be the union of several components. None of the exact metric or neighborhood-boundary inequalities uses connectivity of B. The geodesic predecessor conditions only require a same-side first step or an adjacent anchor, also valid for the union. If one side contains only a low vertex, all three anchors touch it. Its even set covers the opposite odd-depth layers and the anchor even sets cover the even-depth layers, forcing n≤4k+4. For both sides containing a high vertex, high depth is at most three and low depth at most four, by the high-pair and full diameter bounds. There are no restrictions on port supports, presence of nonports, or missing anchor types in this family.

Together with the previous reduction and the κ≤2 results, these computations give traceability at κ≤3 for every k and all orders. As throughout this note, the solver backend is shared by the primary and independent replays.
