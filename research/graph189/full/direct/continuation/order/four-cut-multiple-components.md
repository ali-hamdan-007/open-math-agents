# A four-cut leaving more than two components

When k≥16 the shallow-side theorems apply with a connected shallow component A and B the union of all other components of G minus S. All anchors meet every component by minimality; high opposite distances, distance-to-S, cross-port counts, internal-neighborhood boundary reservations and the matching to B only require separation. Connectivity on the A side is used to handle its possible low nonport. The only step using connectivity of B in order/four-cut-shallow-common-anchor.md was the case of zero deficit d: there a B contact on i has k high A members at distance two. If B consists of several components, an i-contact in another B component is one more even partner, immediately impossible. The depth-three criterion and all-shallow port count use no connectivity of B. Thus any such connected shallow component suffices.

Now assume at least three components and that every one contains a high nonport. Choose three components C_j and an anchor u having high neighbors y_j in all three: if one chosen component contains the low vertex, some high vertex in it touches S (otherwise the sole low vertex would disconnect its high nonport from S), and choose u to meet this high port. Let A_j=N(u)∩C_j and r_j=|N(y_j)∩S|. The closed internal neighborhood of y_j has size ≥k+1−r_j and is contained in A_j together with the members of C_j at distance two from u. Also y_j has every A_l for l≠j at distance two, so Σ|A_j|≤3k/2. Hence

    3(k+1)−Σ r_j ≤ Σ|A_j|+|L₂(u)| ≤ (5/2)k.

Thus Σ r_j≥ceil(k/2)+3≥11 and at least two y_j in distinct components touch all four anchors. Each such vertex sees every port outside its own component at distance two, implying there are at most 2k ports overall. Similarly each of two high nonports belonging to different components sees every high nonport beyond its own component at distance four. At least four more even slots go to the second layer separating it from those fourth-layer vertices. The total number of high nonports is therefore at most 2(k−4). Including the possible additional low nonport and S gives n≤4k−3≤6k−11. This closes the many-component case even in the k=16,17,18 range below the older k>6(s−1) criterion.
