# Minimum exterior degree for every longest cycle

In a hypothetical nontraceable Graph 189 graph with vertex connectivity at least three and second-smallest degree k≥3, take any longest cycle Q. Every vertex of ambient degree at least k outside Q has at least ceil(k/2) neighbors outside Q. In particular no longest cycle can have independent exterior, or exterior consisting of isolated vertices and disjoint edges. This is an assertion about longest cycles, in addition to the analogous earlier assertion for paths.

Index Q cyclically. Write T_x for the set of cycle neighbors of x outside Q, a_x=|T_x| and q_x for its degree outside Q. Consecutive vertices of Q cannot both belong to T_x. The successors and predecessors of T_x are therefore at distance exactly two from x and each set has size a_x.

**An isolated high vertex outside Q.** Suppose x has no outside neighbors. Then a_x≥k and the cap forces a_x=k and the predecessors and successors of T_x are the same k vertices. Hence T_x is invariant under moving two steps round Q. It cannot be all of Q, so the cycle length is 2k, T_x is one parity class, and its complement Y is the other. The entire positive even layer from x equals Y, with all its members at distance two. Every vertex outside Q other than x must be at distance three from x: degree one along a shortest path leads into T_x, distance two is exhausted by Y, and distance four is impossible. It therefore has a neighbor in Y.

In fact no such additional exterior component can exist. Take a component D of the exterior distinct from the singleton x. Only Y can touch D, since membership in N(x) already forbids any D neighbor by the exhausted cap. Two-connectivity supplies distinct attachment vertices u,v in Y, connected by a u-to-v path with all interior vertices in D. Orient Q and write u',v' for their respective successors in T_x. Delete the two directed cycle edges uu',vv', leaving the two disjoint paths from u' to v and from v' to u. Reconnect u to v through D, and connect u' to v' through x. This gives a cycle visiting all of Q, x and at least one member of D, contrary to maximality. Hence x is the only exterior vertex; opening Q at a neighbor of x gives a spanning path. The isolated-high alternative is impossible in a counterexample.

**A high vertex with a high exterior neighbor.** For any such edge xy, T_y is disjoint from both the predecessors and successors of T_x, since the intervening cycle edge could be replaced by x,y. Put r=|(T_x+1) outside (T_x−1)|. Any common contact i in T_x and T_y has i+2 outside T_x, or the segment i,i+1,i+2 could be replaced by i,y,x,i+2. Hence |T_x∩T_y|≤r: map i to its successor in (T_x+1) outside (T_x−1). Besides the a_x+r shifted witnesses, x sees at distance two every member of T_y outside T_x, and every exterior neighbor of y outside N(x) and distinct from x. The latter set has at least q_y−q_x members. The three sets are disjoint, so
```
k ≥ a_x+r + (a_y−r) + q_y−q_x ≥ a_x+k−q_x.
```
Thus q_x≥a_x. Since q_x+a_x≥k, the desired q_x≥ceil(k/2) follows in this branch.

**A high vertex with exactly one exterior neighbor, the unique low z.** Now k−1≤a_x≤k. If a_x=k, or a_x=k−1 with distinct predecessor and successor sets, then either T_x alternates around all of Q or the shifts of T_x consume k slots and T_x is a single step-two run of length k−1. In the alternating case the shifts consume at least k−1 slots as well. When the difference of the two shift sets has size one, there is a single partial run along the step-two permutation. A full orbit cannot coexist with it: for an odd cycle there is only one orbit, and for an even cycle one complete parity class forbids all contacts in the other by nonconsecutiveness. Every T_x vertex therefore has another T_x vertex two steps away in either pattern, since a_x≥k−1≥2. The point z cannot touch any such vertex (lengthen the corresponding two-edge segment through z,x), nor any shifted neighbor of T_x (lengthen the intervening edge using x,z). Any other neighbor of z besides x is therefore a further positive-even witness at x. There is at most one spare slot, so d(z)≤2, contrary to connectivity. The same alternating reasoning applies if a_x=k−1 and both shifted sets coincide. This settles the single-low-neighbor case.


These cases exhaust all high exterior vertices. In a nontraceable graph every cycle omits at least two vertices (a cycle omitting just one can be opened at its neighbor), and therefore at least one high vertex. Its component outside any longest cycle has minimum degree at least ceil(k/2) on every high vertex. This excludes every case in which the exterior of some longest cycle has maximum degree below ceil(k/2); it applies at arbitrary graph order and degree spread.
