# Current replay of the Graph 189 paper

The [paper](full-argument.md) argues that every graph in the exact conjecture has a Hamiltonian path. The [accepted child reduction](https://github.com/lefttosolve2/open-math-agents/pull/18811) reduces the two-connected case to the moderate-order theorem and a large-order theorem with a three-vertex obstruction. The [compiled graph-facing ranges](parent-ranges.md) record which instances already have Lean terms.

The active separating-pair proofs use the [two-cut paper](../sep/unrestricted-two-cuts.md). The [shared-nearest note](../order/two-cut-shared-nearest.md) treats the case of common ports on both deep sides and the case of adjacent anchors. In the section on two deep sides without common ports, the two quota inequalities need to hold for the actual two sides. For the first, sum the two separator vertices' distance-two populations. For the second, take one private contact of each type at distance two from the opposite anchor. The distances from either contact to a vertex of the other side are minima over the two anchors. These two minima count all ports once and common ports twice. Each representative also sees the opposite anchor at distance two. The two exceptional populations in the inequality are precisely vertices at depth two from both anchors and at depth at least three from both. Relating these counts to the side routes and gluing them is still needed.

The [finite-degree note](moderate-degree-six-rotation.md) and [degree-seven note](moderate-rotation-proof.md) give path constructions under independent kernel replay. In the degree-seven moderate case the protected-path insertion theorem [SevenProtected.traceable](../lowblock/Graph189SevenProtected.lean) passed the pinned kernel. For any three matched contacts the stronger protected [path order bound](../verify/Graph189ProtectedTripleFloor.lean) also passed. [AtomNonneighbor](../order/Graph189AtomNonneighbor.lean) supplies the free nonneighbor used in the two-cut minimum-degree repair. The full degree-six and degree-seven moderate cases are not certified by these individual checks.
