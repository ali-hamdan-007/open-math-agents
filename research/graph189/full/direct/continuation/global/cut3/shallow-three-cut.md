# A shallow side at a minimum three-vertex cut

**Computational theorem.** Let G satisfy the original Graph 189 hypotheses with second-smallest degree k≥16. Suppose a cardinality-minimum cut S has size three, leaves two components A,B, and every vertex of A has a neighbor in S. Then G has a Hamiltonian path. Degree spectra, the exceptional low vertex and the sizes of the two sides are unrestricted. The finite calculation uses Z3 on exact rational linear arithmetic and Boolean support variables. It has no end-to-end Lean certificate.

The paper theorem ../five-times-order-unrestricted.md handles n≤5k−3. Assume n≥5k−2. A vertex on side A touching all of S is settled in shallow-universal-port.md; the case where B has high nonports solely nearest each of the three respective anchors is settled in three-nearest-anchors.md. Renumber so no high B nonport has nearest-anchor set {2} at distance two, with anchors numbered 0,1,2. We may omit A types touching all three anchors. In particular A has a high vertex: otherwise A is a singleton, and minimality of S makes it adjacent to all three anchors.

Every high B vertex has S-depth at most two. Otherwise it is at distance at least four, hence exactly four, from every high A vertex; its distance-two layer has at least three vertices as this layer separates it from a nonempty fourth layer. Thus A has ≤k−3 high vertices, whence |A|≤k−2. Every high A member would then have to touch all three anchors to reach ambient degree k. This was excluded.

**If |B|>3k, a step from any vertex of A to a neighbor cannot decrease two anchor distances.** If it did, choose a second neighbor taking a step towards the remaining anchor, possibly the anchor itself. For each b∈B take an anchor realizing d(y,b)=d(y,i)+d(i,b), where y is the original vertex of A; such an i exists because S separates the two sides. One of the two chosen neighbors decreases d(y,i), and therefore decreases d(y,b) by exactly one. At least one of these two distances to b is positive even, since all three roots lie outside B. Three even-distance quotas bound |B|≤3k, a contradiction.

In this large-B case the anchor graph is independent. If anchors i,j were adjacent, an A i-port missing j would shorten both coordinates by stepping to i, which is impossible. Therefore the A ports at i and j coincide. A vertex touching neither cannot neighbor one touching both, since that edge decreases both coordinates. The shared port class is nonempty by cut minimality; connectedness of A makes the entire side touch i and j. Some member also touches the third anchor, contrary to the reduction.

## Exact linear conditions

Write D for the 3-by-3 ambient anchor distance matrix. Every high vertex of either side has a vector v of distances to the anchors. Allow each coordinate 1 through 4 unless the vertex or that anchor is the unique low vertex, in which case 5 is allowed. This uses the known diameter bound five and high-pair bound four. The same bounds apply to the anchor pair distances. Only vectors with |v_i−v_j|≤D_ij≤v_i+v_j are possible.

For each side, vector and degree category (high or the single low), introduce a Boolean flag and a real variable c. An occupied type has c≥1, an unoccupied one c=0; a low occupied type has c=1. The low vertex is absent, on A, on B, or is a specified anchor. Exactly one low count is required when it lies on a side. The parameter k itself is a real variable with k≥16, so the calculation covers every integral k≥16 in one query per anchor geometry. The sum of the counts is at least 5k−5 after removing the three anchors. Every anchor has a contact on both sides by minimality of S.

The following constraints are imposed for each occupied type of root vector v. Indicator tests on the finite vector list are performed before constructing the linear formulas.

1. Across the cut the distance to a type of vector w equals U=min_i(v_i+w_i): every path crosses an anchor and every anchor gives a walk of this length. Types exceeding the applicable high-pair or general diameter bound cannot coexist. All opposite types with U=2 or 4 debit c units of the root's even quota.
2. On the same side the lower bound is L=max_i|v_i−w_i| and the upper bound is min(U,ambient diameter bound for this pair). Equality at 2 or 4 forces that type into the quota. The even coordinates of v already account for the corresponding anchors. The sum of these disjoint charges is at most k. Opposite distance-four types alone also total at most k−3, since any nonempty fourth layer requires a disjoint second layer of size at least three.
3. A possible internal neighbor type must have L≤1. In the large-B branch, an A–A edge must also change at most one coordinate downwards in either orientation by the step lemma above. Include the root itself in the sum of candidate neighbor-type populations. It must have at least k+1−|{i:v_i=1}| candidates when high, or 4−|{i:v_i=1}| when low, from minimum degree at least three. A proposed A port in the large-B case is forbidden when an actual step to one of its incident anchors decreases two coordinates.
4. For any v_j>1 there must be a predecessor on a geodesic to anchor j. This may be an adjacent anchor i with D_ij=v_j−1 or an occupied possible internal neighbor type with j coordinate v_j−1. Other-side vertices cannot be direct neighbors.
5. In the large-B case an internal A path from vector v to w takes at least Σ_i max(v_i−w_i,0) steps by the step lemma, and also at least the reverse sum. If either exceeds two, concrete vertices of these types have disjoint closed internal neighborhoods. When both are high this requires |A|≥2k+2−|{i:v_i=1}|−|{i:w_i=1}|.
6. Each anchor's even quota includes all c at its even coordinates plus the other anchors at positive even distance in D. The two branches require |B|>3k or |B|≤3k respectively.

These constraints relax integral populations to reals and omit some graph conditions. Every candidate graph supplies a feasible point if it has the specified surviving shape.

## Exhaustion and replay

With anchor 2 the absent singleton class, permuting 0 and 1 permits D_02≤D_12. At equality a low at 0 and 1 gives the same case. Enumerate the remaining distance triangles under the applicable pair diameter bounds and six formal low locations (none, A, B, or one of the three anchors). There are 129 large-B cases with independent anchors and 222 small-B cases. All **351** queries returned UNSAT with libz3 4.13.3.0. Four small-B cases that timed out on a short initial pass each returned UNSAT with a 15-second per-case limit. This excludes the last shallow-side configurations.

The complete artifact requires only exact_types.py, check_exact_cases.py and exact-cases.jsonl. Run `python check_exact_cases.py --replay` in their directory; dependencies are Python and the system libz3.so.4. Omitting `--replay` verifies the entire case-index manifest and a positive control. The control is a concrete qualifying graph with k=16: A and B are disjoint 15-cliques, each side has five vertices of each two-anchor support 01,02,12, and the anchors are independent. Its actual BFS distances and even budgets are checked, and inserting its exact counts after removing only the high-order hypothesis gives SAT. The original separate solver logs and a complete replay record are retained alongside these files. The geometry, constraint directions, low handling and case coverage received an independent code and paper audit.
