# The order 5k−3 theorem for all k

Every qualifying Graph 189 graph with n≤5k−3 has a Hamiltonian path. The paper proof for k≥16 is ../five-times-order-unrestricted.md. The prior k≤5 results, full separating-pair and cut-vertex theorems, and the degree-free portion of ../max-degree-plus-two-exception.md reduce the other degrees to 8≤k≤15, κ≥3, n∈{5k−4,5k−3}, and a longest-path exterior clique C on k−2 vertices. The reduction is valid for k≥6 and its residual clique has k−2≥ceil(k/2)+2, forcing k≥8. The maximum-degree assumption in that paper first enters after this precise structural conclusion, apart from deriving the order hypothesis that is assumed here.

There are three external contact vertices A on the longest path; every high C member touches all three, while a possible low C member may miss some. All other external vertices B miss C. Six distinct immediate brackets on the path give two disjoint B contacts for each mark. In particular any subset J of the three marks has at least 2|J| distinct neighbors in B. Write the marks as the anchors and C as the first side. They pairwise have ambient distance 1 or 2 via a high C member. B need not be connected. The separator-walk distance identity and all type inequalities in shallow-three-cut.md remain valid for this union, since there is no edge from C to B. Each anchor has a contact on both sides; C is connected. Since n≥5k−4 and |C|=k−2, |B|≥4k−5>3k. The double-decrease step constraint applies inside C, including its edges to anchors. The auxiliary same-side boundary bound from all-three-cuts.md uses only 3-connectivity of G.

The finite instances set k separately to each integer from 8 to 15. They impose |C|=k−2, all its high vectors equal to (1,1,1), and a possible low C vector with entries one or two. B high vertices may have S-depth up to three and a low B vertex depth up to four; these follow by their distances to a high C member. No missing singleton-nearest class or nonport is imposed on B. Triangles 111,112,122,222 exhaust all anchor geometries up to permutation. Allow the low on either side, any anchor, or absent.
