# Three-vertex connectivity at all orders for k≥16

**Computational theorem.** A finite connected Graph 189 graph with k≥16 and vertex connectivity three has a Hamiltonian path, with no order, maximum-degree, or minimum-degree restriction. The exact calculations below use Z3 4.13.3.0 on Boolean formulas over rational linear arithmetic; they do not constitute an end-to-end Lean certification.

The theorem through n≤5k−3 is in ../five-times-order-unrestricted.md. At larger order any minimum three-cut S={0,1,2} leaves two sides by ../minimum-cut-two-sides.md, because k>6(3−1). If either side is shallow, apply shallow-three-cut.md. When a side contains three high nonports solely nearest each of the three distinct anchors at distance two, apply the paper theorem three-nearest-anchors.md. Otherwise both sides contain a nonport, possibly low, and each side lacks at least one of the three singleton-nearest high classes.

**Depth reduction.** Every high vertex on either side has S-depth ≤2. If both sides contain high nonports, this follows immediately from high-pair distances ≤4: a high depth-three vertex on one side and high nonport on the other would be ≥5 apart. Otherwise one side A has only the low z as nonport; its other vertices are high ports. If a high vertex of B has depth ≥3, then all the high A ports are at distance four and the second layer of that B vertex contains ≥3 vertices. This gives |A|≤k−2. Since some A vertex is high (a singleton nonport cannot constitute a component), equality |A|=k−2 is forced and each high A vertex is adjacent to all S and all other A members. Every B depth-two vertex has distance four from z, so there are ≤k such vertices. Every high A vertex sees at even distance all B ports and all its depth-three vertices. Greater B depths are impossible since every B member is high. Thus |B|≤2k and n≤3k+1. The low vertex's depth is at most three: the opposite side then has a high nonport, and the pair diameter bound for high-low is at most five.

For the remaining cases take the same distance-vector/count constraints as in shallow-three-cut.md, with the following changes. Allow high vectors of depth up to two on BOTH sides, with no restrictions on which anchors the ports touch. The possible low vector has depth at most three. Require a nonport on each side. Fix the missing singleton-nearest class of B to be anchor 2 and independently try all three missing-class choices for A. For |B|≤3k take also |A|≤3k; if either side has more than 3k vertices, give the large side the name B. When |B|>3k, the previous double-decrease exclusion forbids an edge within S: all A ports touching either endpoint would touch both, this class could not neighbor any vertex touching neither, and connectedness would then make all of A touch both, contradicting its nonport.

Add three elementary constraints. Each high anchor has degree at least k (the low anchor at least three), counted from its incident anchor edges and all type populations having coordinate one there. A geodesic between any two nonadjacent anchors has a predecessor adjacent to the first, either another anchor or an occupied type one step closer to the target. Finally, for root x in its side T, the outside boundary of N_T[x] contains at least three vertices, since deleting it separates the nonempty closed neighborhood from vertices of the opposite side. At most t=|N(x)∩S| boundary members are direct neighbors of x. All other boundary members lie at distance exactly two, in T∪S. Thus the opposite even witnesses together with forced distance-four witnesses in T∪S number ≤k−3+t. This strengthens the earlier distance-four separator count. No connectivity of a set of type labels is assumed.

All 1,053 resulting queries are UNSAT: 387 with |B|>3k and 666 with both sides ≤3k, exactly three choices for the missing A anchor for each of the 351 earlier geometries. Seventy-seven initial subsecond timeouts were all resolved by a 20-second per-case retry. The file deep-cases.jsonl contains one terminal result per case. The geometry/encoding differences and low-only nonport case received an independent audit.

To replay use `python check_deep_cases.py --replay` together with deep_types.py, deep-cases.jsonl, check_exact_cases.py and exact_types.py. The distinct positive control consists of two disjoint 17-cliques and three independent anchors. In each clique ten vertices touch all three and seven touch none. The checker independently computes the graph distances, the second-smallest degree 16 and all even quotas, then fixes its exact vector counts and obtains SAT after deleting only the high-order hypothesis.
