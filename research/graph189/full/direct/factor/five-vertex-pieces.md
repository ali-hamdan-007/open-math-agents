# The minimum-cover obstruction has at least six vertices

Suppose n>3k+2, k≥3, and G is a 2-connected qualifying graph with minimum degree at least three. If a minimum cycle-and-edge cover has at least two pieces, some induced piece has at least six vertices, has a chord of its factor cycle, is not Hamilton-connected, and lacks a spanning balanced biclique.

It suffices to supplement chordless-pieces.md with a permissible coloring for chorded pieces of order five. The following elementary fact provides it: a graph containing a spanning five-cycle and at least one chord is Hamilton-connected or has a vertex v of internal degree two admitting a spanning v–w path for every w≠v. If the minimum internal degree is at least three, the Hamilton-connected Ore criterion applies. Otherwise label a vertex of degree two by 4 in cycle order 0,1,2,3,4. The available chords are 02,03,13. Whenever a degree-two vertex has its two cycle neighbors joined by a chord, it has the stated endpoint property. Indeed the other four vertices contain a four-cycle through those two neighbors consecutively. Every possible target on that four-cycle is cyclically adjacent to at least one of the two neighbors, giving a spanning four-cycle path from that neighbor to the target. Prepend the degree-two vertex. If 03 exists this applies to vertex 4. If both 02 and 13 exist, vertex 4 reaches endpoints 0 and 3 by opening the original cycle, and reaches 1 and 2 along 4,0,2,3,1 and 4,3,1,0,2. In the only remaining case there is just one of 02 or 13, and its skipped middle vertex (1 or 2) has degree two and the shortcut property.

For a five-vertex piece of the second kind, use one color for v and one common color for all other ports. Different colors give a spanning path by the endpoint property. Such a piece cannot be Yeo's witness: no exterior component could touch v since it would have to attach at two distinct vertices of this piece but only v has its color. Hence v would have ambient degree two, contrary to hypothesis. Applying the other exclusions and the same properly colored cycle argument proves the result. More generally the same coloring excludes as a witness any piece containing a vertex of internal degree below the ambient minimum which admits a spanning path in the piece to each other vertex.
