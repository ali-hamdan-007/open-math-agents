# Graph 189 rotation results, 28 November 2026

The unrestricted conjecture remains open. All theorems below concern any hypothetical qualifying 2-connected graph and a nonspanning longest path. The cap is on positive even distances, so the vertex itself is excluded. The endpoint counts can be taken within the family obtained by rotations at either end of a single path. Signed review of the current endpoint counts is in `../review/endpoint-growth-audit.md`.

* `even-exterior.md` and `odd-exterior.md` show every omitted component is either a low singleton or has at least ceil(k/2)+2 vertices. The full even cap improves the preceding unrestricted exterior order lower bound by one for every k≥2. The even file also proves 2d_C(x)≥k+f_x+t_x for high omitted x, charging both other components' distance-two vertices and all even vertices at distance at least four.
* `remote-endpoint.md`, `endpoint-matching.md`, `endpoint-linear-growth.md` and `endpoint-equality.md` give successively stronger endpoint counts. With t=|R|−k−1 the result is k≤2t, with equality impossible for t≥3. Thus for k≥6 at least floor(k/2)+1 distinct high attainable endpoints are at graph distance three from any specified high omitted vertex. Existence of any such vertex does not yet yield an extension.
* `endpoint-stability.md` gives estimates when s=2t−k is small, including ≤3s omitted positive even witnesses for every high endpoint, a two-cluster Hamming bound of 16s when t>11s+5, and at least t/2−82s−1 shared blocks of length two in that range. The error terms only give information when their stated inequalities are numerically useful.
* `endpoint-size-growth.md` contains the earlier quadratic estimate and its distinct variant with γ, the size of a smallest closed-neighborhood cover of the exterior by vertices outside R. `common-attachment-clique.md` excludes larger uniform-contact clique exteriors when the degree surplus is at least half the size of the residual even budget, rounded down.
* `Graph189RotationGap.lean` checks the exterior-path length bound for an endpoint chord (SHA-256 `7fd43050c091302619fc053d7a694ce1a9cb9495d5228ab5753b7352f13b665c`). `Graph189EndpointCut.lean` verifies distance-two neighbor transfer and the actual induced deletion cut in endpoint saturation (SHA-256 `b221a6cfc40b0a32ccbe0d1b619dc02a232d5f784b53bc73e52ceda915c5e988`). `Graph189CrossPivot.lean` rules out the two crossing chords from opposite ends of the longest path by constructing, opening and extending a cycle. These are graph-specific components, not whole formal endpoint-count or traceability theorems.
* The pinned compiler version, exact source hashes, direct compile and independent import axiom checks are in `rotation-kernel.log`, `endpoint-cut-kernel.log`, `cross-pivot-kernel.log` and the review directory. The axiom output is propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound for every named component. These paper theorems require no rotation enumeration. Earlier scratch scripts for a now-superseded finite table are unnecessary for archiving or replay.
