# The equality k=2t is impossible for t≥3

Use the notation of `endpoint-linear-growth.md`, in particular t=|R|−k−1, the count h of R-R path edges, the number m of nonsingleton non-R path blocks and j of singletons. Consider a compatible odd pair of high endpoints u,v. At k=2t every inequality in the endpoint estimate is equality. Indeed q_u=q_v=t, m=t (since 2 min(t,m)−m≤t with equality only at t), h=2t, and j=0. For any high endpoint e on any realization, at most t neighbors lie in R and at most one lies in each of the t blocks. Since d(e)≥2t it has exactly t R-neighbors, degree exactly 2t, and touches exactly one tip of every block. The t second vertices of these blocks from e's entries lie at positive even distance and exhaust F_e by |F_e|≤t. All are on P.

Label the two tips of every block. Each high endpoint has a binary word recording its choice of tip for each block. High endpoints at odd distance at least three never share a chosen tip; their words are complete complements. There are at least 3t high endpoints. For each high e at most t high members are adjacent and at most t further high members are at positive even distance, since F_e already uses t places. Thus at least t−1 high vertices have the complementary word. If no low vertex lies in R, there are 3t+1 high members and each occupied word has multiplicity at least t. All occupied words occur in complementary pairs.

There must be precisely one complementary pair whenever t≥3. If no low vertex lies in R, four classes would already require 4t>3t+1. With a low vertex z, the total number is 3t, so four classes require 4(t−1)≤3t, or t≤4. At t=4 four classes would all have size 3; at t=3 they must have sizes 2,2,2,3. There cannot be six classes in either case. Let D be the set of high endpoints adjacent to z or at positive even distance from z. For each e∈D, z uses an R-neighbor slot or an internal even-distance slot from e, so its complementary class has at least t members. Thus D is empty at t=4, and is contained in the single size-two class opposite the size-three class at t=3. In either case |D|<t. No block tip can be adjacent to z: each tip is touched by some high endpoint in each complementary pair, including one outside D, which would thereby be adjacent to z or at distance two from it. Since all neighbors of the attainable z lie on the path and j=0, it must therefore have a high R-neighbor r. This r belongs to D, and each of its t−1 high R-neighbors belongs to D through its common neighbor r with z (or direct adjacency). Hence |D|≥t, a contradiction. This proves the one-pair claim.

There is no G-edge between high endpoints of the two word classes. If e,e′ were adjacent across the classes, all t opposite tips chosen by e′ would belong to F_e, being nonneighbors of e at distance two via e′. Each such tip would have to be the second vertex from its block's e-entry, because the t forced second vertices exhaust F_e. This makes every block an edge. But then all path vertices belong to R or are tips adjacent to a high R endpoint. No omitted vertex can attach to a member of R, nor can one attach to a tip (that would put an omitted vertex in the high endpoint's F_e, which is contained in the path). This contradicts connectedness.

Finally orient a realization from high u to compatible high v at odd distance. Their words are complementary. Every block is traversed from the u-type tip to the v-type tip, since the neighbors of u and v can only be entries in the orientation from their respective endpoints. Between each successive pair of blocks is a nonempty run of R. If this run has no low member it starts with a v-class high member (touching the preceding exit) and ends with a u-class high member (touching the following entry). This requires a forbidden cross-class R-edge. The t−1 disjoint intermediate runs would therefore each contain the unique possible low member of R, impossible for t≥3. This excludes k=2t for all t≥3. In particular at every k≥6 the integer bound is t≥floor(k/2)+1, so each high omitted vertex has more than k/2 distinct high attainable endpoints at distance three.
