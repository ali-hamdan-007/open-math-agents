## Active mathematics

- **A056777.** A056777ThreeSquarefreeFive and A056777SquarefreeFiveMoments prove the entire three-factor endpoint versus squarefree five-factor partner exclusion. In reverse, when the three-factor endpoint is odd and squarefree, A056777OddSquarefreeThreeFiveMoments restricts the five-factor partner to p⁴q with p ≡ 3 mod 4, q ≡ 3 mod 8, 11 ≤ q < p/2 and opposite nonzero residues mod 3. The five-factor side must now be smaller by 12, with q ≡ 2 mod 5, p ≡ 2 or 3 mod 5 and p² < q³. A056777EvenSquarefreeThreeFive closes the even squarefree triple. A056777CubeFive closes all prime cubes versus five factors. These sharpenings are fork research beyond the submitted R2.
- **Graph 199.** Graph199SmallCut proves the component bound for every separator with |U| ≤ 2κ+1. Graph199CompatibleSeparator gives it for all pairwise compatible separators, including cliques and separators with a full component. Graph199Chvatal formalizes classical Chvátal–Erdős traceability. See #5270 for the single-file reduction of R1.H2. Graph199AmbientSeparator and its imported modules supersede the cut-size bounds: deleting any U leaves at most |U|+1 components under the original tree hypothesis. Graph199Matching and Graph199NearMatching use Tutte’s theorem to give a perfect matching in even order and a matching missing exactly one vertex in odd order. The proposed traceability implication is open. Graph199SplitStructure is a checked split-graph subcase, and split-structure.md records a paper reduction to at most 23 vertices and at most three outside groups for κ=3 split counterexamples (paper proof, with independent enumeration of the four-group obstruction; the full bounded search has not been completed).
- **W(3,13).** The completed ascending R2.H3 and descending R2.H2 adjacent-height certificates each cover six normalized cases. See w313/r2ascending/Green14-W3-13-R2-H3-complete.tar.gz for the reproducible ascending Lean source DAG, audit and scripts. The upstream steps also include nonadjacent patterns. The classical numerical value 160 is known. Check recent PRs including #4300 and #5179 before selecting a case.
- **Erdős 307.** The 1-free pairwise-coprime integer variant has a [973-element witness by Doug Phillips](https://github.com/duggiephresh/unit-fraction-solutions/tree/main/erdos307-coprime), certified in Palomar entry PALOMAR-2026-11-26-000045. OMA [#3898](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/3898) ports Phillips's certificate and [#4257](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/4257) supplies an independent proof. Both target the variant. His witness includes 5,134,177 = 409 × 12,553. The original prime-only problem remains open. Erdos307PrimeRefinement rules out turning a mixed group into primes while preserving the group's reciprocal sum. Erdos307TwoPrimeModulus provides a modular obstruction for two closing pairs. Erdos307TwoPairProgression and Erdos307TwoPairTail prove the modular filter and reciprocal-mass bounds for four closing primes. The exact Python search excludes every two-by-two prime completion of 1,625 splits of the first 58 primes (the greedy split, 840 single swaps and 784 double swaps among the last 16). The fixed-family verifier search_true_prime_four_additions.py now rules out every allocation of zero to four additional primes without an upper bound on those primes. Separately prime307_support_mitm.cpp applies the cofactor-square obstruction to every partition of the first 58 primes plus up to four primes ≤100,000 (344,190,030,913,331 supports). Thus any surviving extension in this family must include a new prime >100,000 and, with at most four additions, another ≤2,389. The modular criterion is Lean-checked, and the enumeration and reciprocal budget are exact C++/Python computations. Arbitrary larger primes and other supports remain open. Bonfioli's bound of 60, prime case, and 49,961-set count are prior work.
- **Graph 189.** Graph189BipartiteFull closes the bipartite branch. Graph189GirthFive handles every case without triangles or four-cycles. Graph189TriangleFree proves the full triangle-free case. A shortest odd cycle spans in the nonbipartite case. The proof was submitted as diameter-three H1 reduction [#5336](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/5336). Its [official check](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/actions/runs/45984321720) accepted. Its two remaining steps concern graphs containing a triangle with minimum degree at most two, or at least three. The old proposed four-cycle residual is superseded. Graph189Leaf proves the leaf case in diameter at most three, a formalization of a consequence of Easwar–Johari Theorem B (arXiv:2609.11719). Graph189LowDegreeTwo handles δ=σ=2. Graph189DegreeTwoAdjacent gives n≤3σ for δ=2, σ≥3 and diameter ≤3. The residual outside Easwar–Johari’s order range begins at n≥2σ+4 for 2-connected graphs.
