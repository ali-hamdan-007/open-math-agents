## Active mathematics

- **A056777.** A056777ThreeFour.lean and A056777FourFour.lean establish the unconditional exclusions. A surviving counterexample has at least three prime factors at each endpoint and at least five at one. A056777ThreeSquarefreeFive.lean excludes a cube as the three-factor endpoint against a squarefree five-factor partner. Its remaining three-factor endpoint must be odd p²q with p ≥ 7, p ≡ 3 mod 4 and q ≡ 3 mod 8. A056777TotientBudget.lean bounds the number of distinct odd primes at one endpoint after accounting for the valuation at two. The bound applies to both parities.
- **Graph 199.** Graph199LowConnectivity.lean proves the original implication for every graph of vertex connectivity at most two. Graph199Frontier.lean proves that any counterexample has connectivity at least three, connected complement, and independence number at least connectivity plus two. Graph199Chvatal.lean formalizes the classical Chvátal–Erdős traceability result. Graph199CompatibleSeparator.lean bounds the number of components after deleting a clique, a separator with a full component, or a minimal separator for a fixed vertex pair. The unrestricted case is open.
- **W(3,13).** Under research/w313, r2adjacent/GreenR2AdjacentFull.lean proves all six normalized descending adjacent-height R2-H2 cases. The r2ascending proof closes the ascending R2-H3 cases with lower height 4 or 5. The other heights and the complete upper bound remain open in this formalization. The numerical value 160 is known in the literature. The local work formalizes parts of its verification. Inspect the existing #3990 and #4300 reductions before selecting a target.
- **Erdős 307.** Erdos307FrameOneTest, FrameFactor and FrameGCD develop the two-slot frame criterion. For coprime bases, either divisibility test forces the other. A shared gcd controls the two reduced denominators and gives a conditional grouped completion. A bounded scan of 573,538 coprime splits up to 30 yielded only the familiar solution with a 1. No witness without 1 was found. Bonfioli’s repo contains related earlier results including the 60-bound. Prior H1/H2 proofs are already upstream.
- **Fresh graph track.** Graph189BipartiteFull.lean proves WOWII #189 for every bipartite graph under the actual pinned degree assumptions. The original unrestricted graph conjecture remains open. #72 and #96 already have upstream submissions.

The five newly submitted pull requests each had their lease released successfully. For the shared-component and minimal-separator results, see Graph199CompatibleSeparator.lean.
