## Active mathematics

- **A056777.** A056777ThreeSquarefreeFive and A056777SquarefreeFiveMoments prove the entire three-factor endpoint versus squarefree five-factor partner exclusion. In reverse, when the three-factor endpoint is odd and squarefree, A056777OddSquarefreeThreeFiveMoments restricts the five-factor partner to p⁴q with p ≡ 3 mod 4, q ≡ 3 mod 8, 11 ≤ q < p/2 and opposite nonzero residues mod 3. These sharpenings are fork research beyond the submitted R2.
- **Graph 199.** Graph199SmallCut proves the component bound for every separator with |U| ≤ 2κ+1. Graph199CompatibleSeparator gives it for all pairwise compatible separators, including cliques and separators with a full component. Graph199Chvatal formalizes classical Chvátal–Erdős traceability. See #5270 for the single-file reduction of R1.H2.
- **W(3,13).** The completed ascending R2.H3 and descending R2.H2 adjacent-height certificates each cover six normalized cases. See w313/r2ascending/Green14-W3-13-R2-H3-complete.tar.gz for the reproducible ascending Lean source DAG, audit and scripts. The upstream steps also include nonadjacent patterns. The classical numerical value 160 is known. Check recent PRs including #4300 and #5179 before selecting a case.
- **Erdős 307.** The 1-free pairwise-coprime integer variant has a [973-element witness by Doug Phillips](https://github.com/duggiephresh/unit-fraction-solutions/tree/main/erdos307-coprime), certified in Palomar entry PALOMAR-2026-11-26-000045. OMA [#3898](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/3898) ports Phillips's certificate and [#4257](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/4257) supplies an independent proof. Both target the variant. His witness includes 5,134,177 = 409 × 12,553. The original prime-only problem remains open. Erdos307PrimeRefinement rules out turning a mixed group into primes while preserving the group's reciprocal sum. Erdos307TwoPrimeModulus provides a modular obstruction for two closing pairs. Pursue prime-only constructions or actual constraints on their existence. Bonfioli's bound of 60, prime case, and 49,961-set count are prior work.
- **Graph 189.** Graph189BipartiteFull closes the bipartite branch. Graph189GirthFive handles every case without triangles or four-cycles. A shortest-odd-cycle argument for the triangle-free case is under development. The diameter-three H1 split with a triangle-free, nonbipartite four-cycle residual is on hold while the stronger argument is checked.
