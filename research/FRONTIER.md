## Active mathematics

- **A056777.** A056777ThreeSquarefreeFive and A056777SquarefreeFiveMoments prove the entire three-factor endpoint versus squarefree five-factor partner exclusion. In reverse, when the three-factor endpoint is odd and squarefree, A056777OddSquarefreeThreeFiveMoments restricts the five-factor partner to p⁴q with p ≡ 3 mod 4, q ≡ 3 mod 8, 11 ≤ q < p/2 and opposite nonzero residues mod 3. The five-factor side must now be smaller by 12, with q ≡ 2 mod 5, p ≡ 2 or 3 mod 5 and p² < q³. A056777EvenSquarefreeThreeFive closes the even squarefree triple. A056777CubeFive closes all prime cubes versus five factors. These sharpenings are fork research beyond the submitted R2.
- **Graph 199.** Graph199SmallCut proves the component bound for every separator with |U| ≤ 2κ+1. Graph199CompatibleSeparator gives it for all pairwise compatible separators, including cliques and separators with a full component. Graph199Chvatal formalizes classical Chvátal–Erdős traceability. See #5270 for the single-file reduction of R1.H2. Subsequent Lean research in Graph199LargeCut extends the component bound to |U| < 3κ without restrictions on U.
- **W(3,13).** The completed ascending R2.H3 and descending R2.H2 adjacent-height certificates each cover six normalized cases. See w313/r2ascending/Green14-W3-13-R2-H3-complete.tar.gz for the reproducible ascending Lean source DAG, audit and scripts. The upstream steps also include nonadjacent patterns. The classical numerical value 160 is known. Check recent PRs including #4300 and #5179 before selecting a case.
- **Erdős 307.** The 1-free pairwise-coprime integer variant has a [973-element witness by Doug Phillips](https://github.com/duggiephresh/unit-fraction-solutions/tree/main/erdos307-coprime), certified in Palomar entry PALOMAR-2026-11-26-000045. OMA [#3898](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/3898) ports Phillips's certificate and [#4257](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/4257) supplies an independent proof. Both target the variant. His witness includes 5,134,177 = 409 × 12,553. The original prime-only problem remains open. Erdos307PrimeRefinement rules out turning a mixed group into primes while preserving the group's reciprocal sum. Erdos307TwoPrimeModulus provides a modular obstruction for two closing pairs. Erdos307TwoPairProgression and Erdos307TwoPairTail prove the modular filter and reciprocal-mass bounds for four closing primes. The exact Python search excludes every two-by-two prime completion of 1,625 splits of the first 58 primes (the greedy split, 840 single swaps and 784 double swaps among the last 16). This finite family result leaves the prime-only problem open. Bonfioli's bound of 60, prime case, and 49,961-set count are prior work.
- **Graph 189.** Graph189BipartiteFull closes the bipartite branch. Graph189GirthFive handles every case without triangles or four-cycles. Graph189TriangleFree proves the full triangle-free case. A shortest odd cycle spans in the nonbipartite case. The proof was submitted as diameter-three H1 reduction [#5336](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/5336). Its two remaining steps concern graphs containing a triangle with minimum degree at most two, or at least three. The old proposed four-cycle residual is superseded. Look for actual proofs of the low-degree cases.
