# Independent audit of the connectivity-four finite check

The conclusion established by the finite search is that a connected triangle-free graph with all degrees four or five, an independent set of size six, and no induced seven-vertex tree is bipartite. Excluding order seven also excludes every larger induced tree, by repeated leaf deletion. The exact path child at connectivity four supplies these hypotheses through minimum degree, the induced-star maximum-degree bound, and its independence premise. The bipartite path theorem is a separate result.

## Order and the fixed witnesses

A four-edge geodesic has private leaf sets at its endpoints obtained by omitting neighbors of the middle vertex. No selected leaves are vertices of the geodesic, and triangle-freeness and shortestness exclude all unintended edges. The two neighborhoods of the endpoints are disjoint and at most five of their combined neighbors are omitted, so at least three leaves remain. This gives an induced tree of order eight, bounding diameter by three. For a three-edge geodesic, at most two private neighbors of either endpoint can extend the path as leaves, yielding the stated distance-layer incidence bounds.

For a degree-five root, a distance-two vertex with exactly one first-layer neighbor would give a seven-vertex double star. Thus all second-layer vertices have at least two first-layer neighbors, and the ones touching the third layer have at least three by the geodesic bound. The degree-four and degree-five regular cases then give order at most seventeen from the recorded budgets. For the mixed case, choose a degree-five root with a degree-four neighbor, so the budget is nineteen. With p second-layer vertices that touch the third layer, q other second-layer vertices and r third-layer vertices, 3p+2q≤19 and r≤p. To obtain order eighteen one needs p+q+r=12, hence 2p+q≥12 and 5≤p≤6. The only triples are (5,2,5) and (6,0,6). In the first triple the two q-vertices cannot reach degree four after both incidence budgets saturate. In the second triple the six third-layer vertices form a six-cycle. Deleting one turns it into an induced five-vertex path, and its two second-layer neighbors then attach to that path as nonadjacent leaves. This excludes order eighteen.

The six independent vertices send at least 24 edges to the remaining n−6 vertices, each of degree at most five, so n≥11. In a putative nonbipartite graph an induced five-cycle exists: without one, shared-neighbor pairs give induced double stars and the intersection bound makes “equal or share a neighbor” transitive. Even walks stay in an equivalence class, no edge stays inside a class by triangle-freeness, and connectedness therefore gives a bipartition. An independent set meets an induced five-cycle in at most two vertices. Dihedral relabeling covers the three possibilities by the fixed intersections ∅, {0}, and {0,2}. Together with the arbitrary relabeling off the cycle this covers all 21 witness instances at n=11,…,17.

## Finite encoding and verdict scope

The fixed assignments in finite_k4.py encode simple undirected graphs, the induced labeled cycle and independent set, degree bounds, and triangle-freeness. For any induced path outside the independent set, each independent vertex with exactly one incident edge to the path can be attached as a leaf. The static path clauses are necessary consequences of the seven-tree prohibition. The search only uses paths on two through four vertices, which is safe because all remaining induced seven-trees are handled lazily.

For any permitted relabeling group take a globally lexicographically least edge-bit tuple. Under a transposition a<b of two labels within the same group, the first affected entry that changes compares their adjacency bits at the least differing other vertex. Thus the row comparison at every successive pair of labels is implied by global minimality and every isomorphism orbit retains a representative. Keeping only those comparisons does not assume that they uniquely identify orbits.

A seven-set with six induced edges and connected induced graph is exactly a tree. The lazy witness finder checks both properties and the generated Boolean clause excludes exactly that 21-bit induced pattern. The 400-clause batch cap affects speed only, since any nonempty batch excludes the current assignment. The certificate verifier parses the entire clause, checks that all 21 unordered pairs occur exactly once, and checks the pattern by merging components. The recorded exhaustive query suite consists of the 21 symmetry-reduced instances and reports UNSAT in all cases. These are checked SMT instances using Z3, without a separate solver proof certificate. The optional symmetry-free reruns are an additional check and are not needed for coverage.
